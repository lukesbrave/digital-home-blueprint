# Digital Home Blueprint

The files your AI employee needs to build your Digital Home.

One step. In Telegram, send your agent:

```
/skills install github.com/lukesbrave/digital-home-blueprint
```

Then say: **"start the mission"**

Your employee takes it from there.

---

What's inside:

- `mission-build-home/` — the mission your agent follows to build your
  Digital Home with you, including the identity it takes on
  (`assets/SOUL.md`)
- `digital-home-standup/` — the build engine: clones the latest release of
  the open-source starters, applies your database, deploys your backend,
  and gives you a front door

Everything your agent builds lands on YOUR accounts: your Supabase, your
Cloudflare, your GitHub. That's the point.

Versioned. When we improve the blueprint, reinstall to get the latest.
